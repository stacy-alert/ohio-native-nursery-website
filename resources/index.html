Why Plant Native?

Built for This Climate

Native species are species that occur naturally in our region and have evolved alongside local wildlife, so they're already adapted to Ohio's climate and soil — lower maintenance than typical landscaping plants once established.

Supports Local Wildlife

Native trees, shrubs, and wildflowers feed and shelter the pollinators, birds, and other wildlife that depend on them — a yard of non-natives offers them little.

Simple Early Care

Most natives need proper placement, regular watering through the first year while roots establish, and seasonal pruning after that — we send a care sheet with every purchase.
