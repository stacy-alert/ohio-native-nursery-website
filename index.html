Why Plant Native?

Built for Ohio

Native species are already adapted to our soil, rainfall, and temperature swings — less watering, less fuss, once established.

Support Local Wildlife

Native trees, shrubs, and wildflowers feed and shelter the pollinators, birds, and beneficial insects that depend on them.

Grown Locally

Every plant is grown right here, so what you buy is ready for the conditions in your own yard.
