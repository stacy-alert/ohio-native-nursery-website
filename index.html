Frequently Asked Questions

What types of native plants do you offer?

Our nursery specializes in native plants that are adapted to Ohio's climate and soil conditions. We focus on species that enhance local ecosystems and provide habitat for wildlife, ensuring a sustainable gardening experience.

What is the importance of using native plants?

Using native plants is crucial for maintaining local biodiversity, supporting pollinators, and restoring natural habitats. They require less water and maintenance, making them an eco-friendly choice for landscaping.

Do you offer resources for landscape professionals?

Yes, we provide a range of resources for landscapers and conservation groups, including plant selection guides, habitat restoration tips, and information on sustainable gardening practices to support your projects.

How can I get updates on plant availability?

You can subscribe to our newsletter for the latest updates on plant availability, new arrivals, and special promotions. We also post regular updates on our website to keep you informed.

What are the benefits of rewilding my garden?

We offer a diverse selection of native trees, shrubs, and plants well-suited for Ohio's ecosystems. Our catalog includes species that promote biodiversity and support local wildlife, ensuring that your garden thrives while contributing to environmental conservation.

How can I care for my native plants effectively?

Caring for native plants involves understanding their specific needs, such as soil type, sunlight, and watering requirements. We provide detailed care guides for each species in our catalog to help you maintain a healthy garden.
