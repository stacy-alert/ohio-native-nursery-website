About Us

Ohio Native Nursery is a tree farm established by Patrick Murphy, a lifelong gardening enthusiast dedicated to planting and caring for Ohio's native trees, shrubs, and wildflowers.

At Ohio Native Nursery, we believe in the power of native plants to restore habitats, support pollinators, and protect biodiversity. Every tree and plant grown here is carefully selected to thrive in Ohio's landscapes and help rebuild the connections between people, wildlife, and the land.

Our nursery is about more than selling plants — it's about restoring the natural beauty and balance of our region.

How We Grow

Locally Sourced

All our trees, shrubs, and wildflowers are grown locally in Cincinnati from regionally sourced seed or cuttings, so they're already adapted to our weather and soils.

Pesticide-Free

We avoid harmful chemicals that could harm pollinators or soil health. Our growing practices focus on healthy, sustainable soil and pest management.

Grown to Last

Every plant leaves with a care sheet — proper placement, watering through the first year, and seasonal pruning, so it actually thrives once it's in your yard.
