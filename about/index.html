About Ohio Native Nursery

Growing Ohio's native trees, shrubs, and wildflowers.

Ohio Native Nursery was started by Patrick Murphy, a lifelong gardening enthusiast, to make it easier for homeowners, landscapers, and land managers to find quality native trees, shrubs, and wildflowers suited to Ohio's climate and soil.

We focus on species that support local ecosystems — providing food and habitat for pollinators, birds, and other wildlife — while being genuinely well-suited to backyards, restoration sites, and everything in between.

Our current plant availability is listed on the Plant Inventory page, updated regularly as stock changes throughout the season.
